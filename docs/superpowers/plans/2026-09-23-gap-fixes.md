## Rulings

- **R-1 Spec-deferred, not gaps:** subtitle sync (`SyncSpec`), Whisper
  generation (`WhisperSpec`, and so the `whisper` provider) and chunked
  transcoding are marked *deferred* in the design of record. They stay
  CEL-forced off. Implementing them would contradict the spec.
- **R-2** Usenet cross-indexer dedupe stays per-indexer (spec §6.2 names
  exactly two keys; a colliding key loses releases). Closed by design.
- **R-3** Season pack vs single-episode target: follow Sonarr's
  `SingleEpisodeSearchMatchSpecification` — an automatic *single-episode*
  search rejects a full-season pack; a season search and the RSS path keep
  accepting packs that cover wanted episodes. Verify the Sonarr source first.
- **R-4** Subtitles: Bazarr pooling — search every eligible provider, score
  all candidates together, take the best. First-provider-wins goes.
- **R-5** `status.activeDownloadRef` gets one writer: the Movie/Episode
  reconcilers (`ManagerCatalogarr`), deriving it level-style from the
  item's owned non-terminal `Download`. The grab worker stops writing it; its
  double-grab guard looks up existing Downloads for the target instead.
- **R-6** Engine teardown race: each engine adds its own finalizer to a
  Download it owns, removes the transfer, then drops it; the controller's
  `removeDataOnDelete` finalizer runs `SafeRemove` only after the engine's
  finalizer is gone, or after a bounded timeout when the engine is gone.
- **R-7** `SecondaryYear`: add it; populate where the provider exposes one;
  identity accepts Year, SecondaryYear, or the existing ±1 tolerance.
- **R-8** Only the three named `omitempty` scalars become pointers
  (`CRFTable.HDROffset`, `IndexerSpec.MinimumSeeders`,
  `RecycleBin.CleanupDays`); the other 41 have meaningless zeros — closed.
- **R-9** `IndexerProxy.spec.port` becomes `+required`.
- **R-10** A non-video import list whose upstream cannot yield that kind is
  rejected at admission by CEL, not skipped with a log line; one that can is
  supported.
- **R-11** `replaceSource: false` and the container change (R8 of Phase E)
  are implemented, not deferred — both are v1 spec values. X10 designs the
  output location from spec §9 and records its ruling.
- **R-12** Items that only asked for a confirmation (`WithSeasons`,
  `preferLargest` 0.99, anacrolix workarounds, `DownloadOverlay`,
  `DownloadPriority` mapping) are closed with evidence, or fixed if the
  evidence says so.
- **R-13** Cardigann corpus: vendor the upstream Prowlarr definitions at a
  pinned commit **only if its licence is GPL-3.0-compatible** (verify and
  cite); ship the sync script regardless. *Applied at X8a: nothing was
  vendored; `hack/sync-cardigann` and `cardigann.LoadBundle` shipped, and
  the licensing record is in `hack/sync-cardigann`'s package doc.*
  **Superseded by the project owner on 2026-09-23 (`7b6fc4a`):** the owner
  added Prowlarr's Cardigann definitions to the project (752 files), which
  `hack/pack-cardigann` packs into `app/indexer/bundle/embedded/definitions.zip`
  and indexarr loads at startup (`--cardigann-bundled`, default true;
  `--cardigann-definitions-dir` replaces it).
